2026-10-11  Foundation for five languages (English unchanged, nothing else live)

Request (owner): the site readable in English, Spanish, Chinese, Swahili
and French, with a language menu; chat replies in the tenant's language;
statutes stay in official English.

Owner's answers to the brief's questions (2026-10-11, "do recommended"):
a fluent reader reviews legal wording per language before it goes live;
first visit follows the browser's language; sign-in emails stay English
for now; Chinese is both Simplified and Traditional.

This is the first slice. It ships no translation.

Changed
- New dependency: Flask-Babel (and Babel, pytz with it).
- i18n.py: the six language definitions, LIVE_LANGUAGES = English only,
  choosing the language (account, then device cookie, then browser, then
  English), the POST /language switch, gettext helpers that fall back to
  English without setup, a date formatter, and an extractor that reads
  module constants by value.
- Every page: <html lang> follows the choice; a slot for the language
  menu (templates/_language.html), which renders nothing while only one
  language is live.
- Translatable now: branding.py wording (translated where it is injected
  or passed to a page), the password rule messages, all 86 flash messages
  in routes.py (the 11 f-strings became placeholder messages), the privacy
  page, and dates.
- translations/: messages.pot (123 messages, 65 marked REVIEW) and empty
  catalogues for es, fr, sw, zh_Hans, zh_Hant.
- The privacy page gained "Your language": a choice is remembered on the
  device and on the account.
- Sign-in adopts the language saved on the account.

Choices made
- Module-level wording stays plain English strings and is translated at
  the point of use, instead of lazy translation objects, because ~570
  existing assertions and several callers compare those strings.
- The switch has no CSRF token; it checks the request's origin, like the
  click counter, so the menu sets no cookie on public pages until someone
  actually chooses.
- A language is gated in code, not by configuration: adding it to
  LIVE_LANGUAGES is the act of going live, and tests refuse a live
  language with unreviewed wording and a non-live catalogue with any
  translation in it.

Evidence
- tests/test_i18n.py, 31 tests: English-only behaviour unchanged; the
  order of choice; browser lists including Chinese scripts; the switch
  (cookie attributes, own-pages only, safe return address, saved to the
  account, no account touched when signed out, real app needs no token);
  pages, the menu, flash messages and dates in Spanish from a throwaway
  catalogue; official names and URLs left alone; catalogue rules.
- Full suite on the Mac: 1112 OK (1 skipped); 1081 on main before, with no
  existing assertion changed except one list of privacy section titles.
- Headless Chrome with preview languages on: the menu in the header at
  1280 px (light) and 390 px (dark).

Not done, and known
- Template text, JavaScript strings, JSON error messages and messages
  raised in other modules are not wrapped yet. With a language previewed,
  only the items listed above change; the rest of each page is English.
- At 390 px the menu pushes "Sign up free" off the header on pages with a
  full nav. Invisible today (the menu is hidden); must be fixed before a
  language goes live.
- No translation exists. No fluent reader has been involved yet.
- Keyboard use of the menu was not driven by hand (a native select and
  button).
