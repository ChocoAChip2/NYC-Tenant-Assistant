2026-10-01  Existing accounts are asked for new information; citations read properly; the RA-81 is filled completely

Found while testing the live site signed in (the owner's account)
- The account was made before sign-up asked for a name, so chat greeted
  it by its email handle, and nothing would ever ask for the name.
- A grounded answer ended in a bare "[S5]": the per-request marker for
  "passage 5" was stored and shown as-is.
- Reading the RA-81 flow end to end:
  - Only 6 of the form's ~60 fields were ever filled.
  - The landlord's column, rent-regulation status and the notice letter
    were never asked.
  - Every problem went on the "Other" line.
  - That line holds about 45 characters, but 120 were written into it,
    so most of a complaint was clipped out of sight on the printout.

1. Account requirements (account_requirements.py, /account/complete)
- A feature that needs information older accounts lack declares it once.
  The account is asked at its next login. A session that was already
  signed in is asked on its next visit to chat, because the requirements
  carry a version.
- The name and date of birth is the first requirement. It is required:
  the age check is the point.
- Never a dead end:
  - not asked when it can't be stored (no encryption key);
  - a failed save lets the tenant continue, and they're asked again next
    login;
  - an unreadable account isn't gated;
  - optional requirements have "Remind me next time";
  - Settings, public pages and logout are never gated.
- Docs: docs/frontend/account-requirements.md. HANDOFF section 6 makes it
  a rule for every feature.

2. Citation markers
- citation_guard.resolve_markers turns "[S5]" into "(NYC Admin Code
  § 27-2029)" linked to the official text, before the reply is stored or
  shown. Markers that match no passage are dropped.
- Replies stored before this have markers stripped when displayed.

3. RA-81 accuracy (form_service.py, ai_service.py prompt, routes.py)
- Every field is mapped by widget position. The mapping is checked by
  tests that pin positions and order, and was confirmed by filling a
  sample and looking at both pages:
  - tenant and owner columns and phones;
  - regulation status;
  - co-op/condo and 7A;
  - move-in date and number of apartments;
  - SCRIE/DRIE, Section 8 and voucher number;
  - the notice letter's date and method;
  - a checkbox and two lines for each room.
- Nothing is guessed. Unknown or invalid choices and dates stay blank, and
  a copied "yes | no" placeholder is not a choice.
- Nothing is cut off. Text is wrapped with real Helvetica widths, and
  anything that doesn't fit says "continued on attached page" and is
  printed in full on a page appended to the form.
- Appearance streams are written by the app with a font size that fits
  each box. The pypdf version in use escaped parentheses twice
  ("\(rear\)") and couldn't shrink text. NeedAppearances is off, so
  Poppler-based viewers no longer draw empty checkboxes without their
  squares.
- The prompt:
  - collects every field;
  - checks that the apartment is rent-regulated, and points to HHW-1 or
    RA-84 when those apply;
  - asks about the written notice to the landlord;
  - forbids guessing;
  - confirms a summary with the tenant before handing off.
- The route checks the hand-off (missing_fields). If anything DHCR needs
  is missing, the tenant is asked for it and no PDF is made.
- The stored message is the next steps (sign, attach the letter and proof
  of mailing, where to send it), not the raw JSON with the tenant's
  details. The PDF is built in memory, and the temporary file is removed.
- The old {name, address, complaint} hand-off still fills correctly.
