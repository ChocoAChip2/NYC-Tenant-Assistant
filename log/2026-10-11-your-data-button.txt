2026-10-11  "Your situation" becomes an AI-filled "Your data" button in the message box

Request (owner, 2026-10-11): the big "Your situation & reply preferences"
form above each chat goes. In its place, a small "Your data" pill in the
composer's toolbar, left of Send, that opens a popover with things to do.
The assistant fills the summary from what the tenant writes; the tenant
never has to type into a form, but can correct it.

Changed
- Composer: a toolbar row under the textarea with "Your data" (ID-card
  icon, tooltip on hover and focus) and Send. The form above the transcript
  and its template (_situation.html) are removed. New _your_data.html.
- Popover (bottom sheet at 700 px and below): one-line summary with blanks
  hidden; Reply language and Answer length as pick lists that save at
  once; Edit details; Clear details with a confirmation; this chat's RA-81
  drafts when there are any; "Manage all your data" to Settings.
- Keyboard: Enter/Space opens, arrows move between rows, Escape goes back
  or closes and returns focus to the button.
- Filling it in: after a reply arrives the page calls a new route,
  POST /conversations/<id>/situation/refresh. It sends a small model call
  the summary so far and the tenant's unread messages only (never the
  assistant's replies) and saves what passes case_context validation.
- A fact the tenant edits is never overwritten by a later update. Clear
  details keeps the reply settings and does not refill from old messages.
- No send gate: chat sends whether or not the details have loaded.
- No database change. The tenant-set fields and the read-message count
  live inside the same encrypted case_context value.
- The chat-history export heading is now "Your data for this chat".

Decisions taken while building (planner defaults kept unless noted)
- Reply language is not guessed by the extraction: the existing default,
  "Match my message", already replies in the language the tenant writes
  in, until they pick one.
- The extraction runs in its own request after the reply, not inside the
  chat request, so the reply is never slower. It has the chat route's
  limit and asks the model at most once per tenant message.
- An update can add or replace a fact but never blank one.

Tests changed or replaced (tests/test_case_workspace.py)
- test_clear_removes_facts_and_preferences -> replaced by
  test_clear_removes_facts_and_keeps_reply_preferences (Clear now keeps
  language and length) and test_saving_blank_values_still_blanks_everything
  (the old request shape still works).
- No test asserted the send gate; YourDataPageTests now asserts it is gone.
- tests/review_support.py: the in-memory service stores tenant_set/seen and
  the stand-in assistant gained extract_case_details.
- New: YourDataExtractionTests (12), YourDataPageTests (5),
  ExtractionCallTests (2), two storage tests.

Evidence
- Full suite on the Mac: 930 tests OK (1 skipped); 908 before.
- Headless Chrome on the Mac against the in-memory app, light and dark, at
  1280 px and in a 390 px frame: no form above the transcript; button,
  tooltip, popover, language list, edit form and clear confirmation
  rendered; no horizontal overflow.
- Scripted in the same browser: tooltip visible on focus and hidden while
  open; open moves focus to the first row; Down/Up/Home/End move and wrap;
  Escape closes and returns focus to the button, and goes back from a
  sub-view; picking "Español" saved and showed "Saved. Your next reply will
  use it."; an edit of borough to Queens survived the next update, which
  did add "Called 311"; clicking outside closed it.

Not verified
- No real model call was made: extraction quality (what the model actually
  pulls out of a message, and that it leaves suggestions alone in its own
  wording) is untested. The structural guarantees above are tested.
- Enter/Space on the button and real hover were not driven; they rely on
  native button behaviour and a CSS :hover rule.
- Not checked on a real phone or on Render.
