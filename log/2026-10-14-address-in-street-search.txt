2026-10-14  A whole address typed into the street search now works

Requirement (owner, 2026-10-14): "when I enter my address it doesn't work
and defers to error and more information needed; debug and correct this",
read against the 2026-10-07 requirement (street + borough is the default
search, the exact address optional).

What was wrong (live, 2026-10-14, signed out)
- "231 Echo Place, Bronx, NY 10457" in the street box with Bronx chosen:
  404 "We couldn't find that street". The borough, "NY" and the ZIP were
  read as part of the street name.
- The same with the borough menu left empty: 400 "Choose a borough",
  though the text names the borough and its ZIP.
- "231 Echo Place Apt 3B": 404. "37-02 82nd Street, Jackson Heights, NY
  11372" with Queens: 404.
- "37-02 82nd Street", Queens: 503 "The city's data service didn't
  respond" on the first try (a street-wide query passed the 6 s limit),
  200 on the second.
- The address search page (/building) was not the problem: 35 address
  formats, including apartments, ZIPs, Queens hyphens and neighbourhood
  names, all geocoded to a building against the live service.

Changed
- street_suggestions.read_street_search: street, borough, ZIP and house
  number out of whatever was typed (see docs/frontend/area-lookup.md).
- routes._render_area_lookup uses it: the menu wins, the text fills in a
  missing borough, a ZIP from the text narrows but falls back to the whole
  street, and a house number adds "Your building: ..." linking to /building.
- building_service: street-wide NYC Open Data queries get 15 s.

Evidence
- tests/test_address_did_you_mean.py: +11 tests (reader cases, route
  cases for each failure above, the timeout rule). Full suite 1156 OK
  (1 skipped).
- Live services, through the real route on the Mac before merging: see
  the PR.

Not changed
- /building, the borough menu's "required" attribute (the server now
  copes when it is left empty by non-browser clients and shared links).

Follow-up, 2026-10-15 (live recheck after the merge)
- All 10 street-box cases on Render returned the right street, borough and
  "Your building" link; plain street and typo unchanged.
- /building for "231 Echo Place, Bronx, NY 10457" was a 503 "The city's
  data service didn't respond" after 12.9 s, then 200 three times in a
  row. On the Mac its per-building count query took 3.5 s, so from Render
  it can pass the 6 s limit. Every NYC Open Data query now gets 15 s (not
  only street-wide ones); the geocoder keeps 6 s.
