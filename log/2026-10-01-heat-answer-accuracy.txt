2026-10-01  The heat answer gave an out-of-date number

Found testing the live site (signed in, after #45)
- Asked: "How warm does my apartment have to be at night in the winter?"
- The search returned 27-2033.1 and 27-2028, not § 27-2029 (Minimum
  temperature to be maintained). The grounding rules then allowed an
  uncited answer "from general knowledge", and the model gave the pre-2017
  rule: 55°F at night when it is below 40°F outside. The law since 2017 is
  62°F overnight, whatever the outside temperature.
- The citation itself rendered correctly (the [S5] fix in #44): "(NYC
  Admin Code § 27-2033.1)", linked to the official text.

Fix
- Search: tenant words warm, warmth, degrees, thermostat and chilly now
  map to heat/temperature. Migration 20261001_search_synonyms_warmth.sql
  (data only) was checked in a rolled-back transaction and then applied
  live. § 27-2029 now comes back for the question above and two
  variants, which were added to tests/fixtures/tenant_questions.json.
- Prompt: specific legal numbers (temperatures, deadlines, dollar
  amounts, percentages, notice periods) are stated only when a source in
  the conversation gives them. Otherwise the rule is described without the
  number, with where to confirm it. The same rule is in the grounding
  block that sits next to the sources (retrieval_service.format_for_prompt).
- Also in this branch: a failed account check (as happened when the
  expired-token bug hit it) now retries 10 minutes later instead of
  waiting for the next login.
