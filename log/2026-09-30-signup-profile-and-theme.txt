2026-09-30  Sign-up asks for name and date of birth; every page follows the browser theme (AMOLED dark)

Asked for
- Sign-up asks for first name, last name and date of birth, and makes
  clear the details are kept confidential.
- The theme follows the browser: light by default, dark when the device
  asks for it, with dark as AMOLED black.
- Chosen: dark = pure black (#000); minimum age 13.

Sign-up profile
- profile_service.py validates the three fields. Names can use letters in
  any script plus spaces, hyphens, apostrophes and periods, up to 50
  characters. The date of birth must be a real date, not in the future, at
  least 13 years ago (Feb 29 handled) and at most 120.
- The profile is stored ENCRYPTED as one envelope in the new account's
  Supabase user_metadata ("profile"). There is no session at sign-up and
  no service-role key, so account metadata is the only place it can go.
  With no DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEYS the profile is not stored at all. It is
  never stored in the clear, even if encrypt() ever returned plaintext.
- At login the first name is decrypted into the session. The chat
  greeting uses it in the Jinja heading and in the time-of-day script. The
  script used to overwrite the heading with the email handle, so the name
  would never have shown. Accounts made before this change greet by the
  email handle as before.
- The date of birth is only checked at sign-up. Neither field reaches the
  AI. Both are deleted with the account (auth.users purge).
- The page: a lock-icon note under the fields, "Your details stay
  confidential", listing exactly the above. The date picker is limited to
  13-120 years old, with autocomplete given-name/family-name/bday. On an
  error the form keeps everything typed except the password.

Theme
- All ten page templates: dark tokens under
  @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) :root:not([data-theme="light"]) and
  the same tokens under :root[data-theme="dark"]. --bg #000000,
  --surface #0a0a0a, borders and tints darkened to match.
- The auth pages (login, signup, forgot/reset password) were light-only.
  They now have the dark tokens and the pre-paint theme script, and
  hard-coded #fff text became var(--on-accent).
- Every page has <meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark"> and
  theme-color metas (#eef1f5 / #000000).
- Settings' Light / Dark / Match system choice still wins over the browser.
- Dark text tokens are AA against both #000 and #0a0a0a (checked in tests).

Checked
- tests/test_signup_profile_and_theme.py (30 tests): name and date rules,
  including the turning-13-today and leap-day edges; the metadata is
  ciphertext only; garbage metadata never breaks login; an under-13
  sign-up never calls Supabase; typed values are escaped when echoed; the
  greeting; and on every page, dark = #000, the two dark blocks match, the
  theme script runs before the styles, and AA contrast.
- Full suite: 690 OK.
- Chromium at 375 px, light and dark: the sign-up page has no horizontal
  scroll, the body is rgb(0,0,0) in dark, an under-13 date is refused with
  the form kept, and chat shows "Good afternoon, Ana".

Needed on Render
- DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEYS must be set, or the profile is not stored. The age
  check still runs either way.
