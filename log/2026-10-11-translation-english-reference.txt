2026-10-11  English as the reference on translated pages

Request (owner): translations are machine-made, so every translated page
says so and links to the same page in English, and legal wording shows
its English original under the translation. Step 2 of the five-language
brief. English is unchanged and no other language is live.

Changed
- A one-line notice at the top of every translated page: "This page was
  translated by a machine. The English page is the reference." with a link
  to the same page in English. Nothing on an English page.
- The link is the page's own address with ?lang=en: one request, not
  remembered, no JavaScript, no form, no cookie. ?lang= accepts live
  languages only.
- i18n.with_original(): the translation with its English original beneath
  it, smaller and marked lang="en". Used for every legal string branding
  injects, the privacy page, the full disclaimer points, the help-resource
  descriptions, the password checklist and its two refusal messages, and
  the RA-81 review screen's labels and group names.
- The chat script builds the per-message note from the translated text and
  the English original as two values.

Evidence
- tests/test_i18n.py: the notice and its link appear on a translated page
  only, above the header, and keep the page's query; ?lang=en gives English
  for that request and sets no cookie; a non-live or unknown ?lang= is
  ignored; legal wording renders as translation plus English original;
  every high-stakes string on the privacy, Learn more and sign-up pages
  goes through the helper; values filled into such a message are escaped
  in both lines; the review screen's labels use it; the chat script gets
  the two values.
- Full suite on the Mac: 1128 OK (1 skipped); 1122 before.
- Headless Chrome with preview languages on: the notice at 390 px (dark)
  and 1280 px (light).

Not verified
- With empty catalogues there is nothing translated to look at, so the
  English originals were seen only through the throwaway catalogue in the
  tests, not on a real page.
- The notice sits above the chat page's full-height layout; that page was
  not looked at with it.
