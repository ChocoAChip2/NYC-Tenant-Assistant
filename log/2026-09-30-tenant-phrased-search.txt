2026-09-30  Tenant-phrased legal search (migration 20260930_tenant_phrased_search)

Problem
- After the library loaded, everyday questions found no law. Full-text search
  required every word, and tenants and the Admin Code use different words:
  landlord/owner, lawyer/counsel/"legal services", roaches/cockroaches,
  bed bugs/bedbug, voucher/"lawful source of income".
- Measured on tests/fixtures/tenant_questions.json: recall@6 was 8 of 38
  questions (6/23 on the tuning set, 2/15 on the held-out set).

Fix (the search_legal_documents contract is unchanged, so the app needed
no change)
- legal_search_synonyms: a public-read table mapping tenant words to law
  words or exact phrases. It is data, so it can grow without a migration.
- Filler words ("apartment", "get", "section", single digits, single
  letters) are dropped.
- Each query word becomes a group (the word OR its law words). Sections
  are scored by the groups they match, each weighted by rarity (IDF), plus
  the same weight again when the group is in the section title. A section
  needs two groups, or its only group, or one group that is in its title.
- One chunk per section. A quoted phrase still matches exactly first.
- Tenant text reaches to_tsquery only as quoted lexemes or through
  phraseto_tsquery, so "a & b | !c" is words, not operators.

Result, on a local copy of the real 308-section library
- Tuning set: 23/23.
- Held-out set (written after tuning, never tuned on): 13/15. The misses:
    "owner turned off the gas and heat so we would leave" -> heat sections, not 26-521
    "can I get a free attorney if I'm being evicted" -> 26-408 Evictions and 26-1304, not 26-1302
- Noise: "hello", "what's the weather", "capital of france" (0-2 results)
  and "file my taxes" (6 results from the tax sections). Leftover keyword noise.
- Speed: about 80 ms median as the anon role.

Live, 2026-09-29 evening
- The rolled-back live test matched the local results.
- Applied as migration "tenant_phrased_search". get_advisors: the only new
  finding is legal_search_synonyms visible in GraphQL, which is intended
  (it is public data).
- The library checks file now checks amended chunks directly. Search now
  matches most-but-not-all words, so the amended heat text ("degrees
  Fahrenheit") legitimately still appears in search results. The checks
  pass with and without the new migration.

Measure again at any time, read-only, with the anon key:
    SUPABASE_URL=... SUPABASE_KEY=<anon> python -m tools.corpus.eval_search --min-recall 0.8

Still true: there are no embeddings yet. Paraphrases that share no words
with the law (e.g. "it's like an icebox in here") still need vector search.
