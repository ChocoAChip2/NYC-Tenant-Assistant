Decisions that changed (owner, 2026-10-11, after the foundation merged)
- Machine translation, with no fluent-reader approval gate. English stays
  the reference: translated pages will say so and link to English, and
  high-stakes wording will show its English original. REVIEW in the
  catalogues now means "shown with its English original".
- Simplified Chinese only. zh_Hant stays defined, never live, catalogue
  empty. A browser asking for Traditional Chinese gets Simplified.
- Sign-in, confirmation, reset and code emails stay English.

  aria-label, title, alt and chip prompt goes through _(). About 470
  strings were wrapped by a one-off script and the rest by hand: plurals
