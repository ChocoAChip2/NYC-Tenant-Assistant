2026-10-07  Street + borough search (front page) and a Resources tab

Request (owner)
- Don't scare people off by asking for an exact address. Street + borough
  is the default; exact address optional. Long streets: ask for a ZIP
  code or cross street. Show the area's representatives and districts
  (looked up behind the scenes). Re-check officials after elections,
  about a month after results, leaving room for runoffs.
- A read-only resources tab now; community posting waits for a layout.

What
- area_service.py: street + borough -> the city's spelling of the street
  (fuzzy matches rejected), every lot on it, districts (Council,
  community, Assembly, State Senate, Congress), officials, community
  board, land use / homes / zoning, and its buildings with open HPD
  violations (including corner buildings HPD files under the street).
  ZIP narrows by lot; a cross street keeps lots within ~160 m of the
  corner. Real data captured for Echo Place, Bronx is the test fixture
  (it crosses Council 14/15 and State Senate 32/33).
- "/" and "/area": the new search page (area.html). "/building" keeps the
  exact-address lookup; old "/?address=" links still work.
- "/resources" (resources.html, resources.py): borough picker, community
  board, Housing Court, free help, where to report.
- Elected officials: supabase/migrations/20261007_elected_officials.sql
  (public read; writes only via token-checked officials_replace()),
  tools/officials/refresh.py (Council from NYC Open Data; Assembly and
  State Senate incumbents from the NY Senate API), and
  .github/workflows/refresh-officials.yml (Dec 15, Jan 15, Apr/Jul/Oct 15;
  opens an issue listing every change).
- Nav: "Resources" in the header of the public pages; chat's empty state
  gets "Look up my street" and "Free help near me".

Fixed along the way
- The chat's referral list (branding.HELP_RESOURCES) gave tenants a "NY
  State Homeowner & Tenant Helpline, (855) 466-3456". That number is the
  State's Homeowner Protection Program, not a tenant line. Replaced with
  the State rent office (HCR, 1 (833) 499-0343) and added the Met Council
  on Housing hotline ((212) 979-0611). Both checked on the agencies' pages.
