2026-10-11  "Did you mean" for the street and address searches, and a wrong-building fix

Request (owner): when a street or address search finds nothing, or finds
something other than what was typed, show up to three clickable
suggestions instead of a dead end, and never show a different building's
violations without saying so. Deterministic only, no model call. Never
redirect.

The bug this fixes
- "231 Ecco Place, Bronx" geocoded to 231 BROWN PLACE and /building showed
  Brown Place's violations as if they were the tenant's. It is now a 404
  with "Did you mean 231 Echo Place, Bronx".

Changed
- New street_suggestions.py: reads a typed address (number, street,
  borough), decides whether a geocoder result is the address that was
  typed, and builds suggestions from the city's street list for the
  borough (HPD buildings dataset, one fetch per borough, kept a day).
- building_service.geocode checks every result against what was typed:
  same street and number is shown; same street with a different number is
  shown with a notice ("We couldn't find 1 Echo Place. Showing 179 Echo
  Place.") and a link to the street; a different street, or a borough
  other than the one typed, is not found.
- Area search: a street that isn't found gets up to three suggestions,
  with the right borough first when the name exists elsewhere.
- Building search: a corrected address with the same house number, shown
  only if it geocodes exactly, plus a link to the street's buildings. If
  that number isn't on the street, the street alone is offered.
- Status codes are unchanged (404). Suggestions are links, never a
  redirect.
- The chat hand-off says "I looked up ..." rather than "I live at ..."
  when a different number on the street is being shown.
- GeoSearch test fixture gained housenumber, street and match_type.

Where the live services differed from the brief
- match_type is "fallback" on every result, including exact matches
  ("231 Echo Place, Bronx"), so it cannot tell a substitution from a hit.
  The street and house number are compared instead.
- The city's street list and the geocoder both carry alias spellings ("GR
  CONCOURSE", "GRANDCONCOURSE") that resolve as streets but have no tax
  lots. resolve_street alone confirmed them, so "Grand Concorse" first
  offered three names, two of them dead ends. A suggestion now also has
  to have buildings.
- A result in another borough than the one typed was also being shown
  ("12A Echo Place Bronx" came back as 12 Echo Place, Staten Island). That
  is treated like a different street.

Choices made
- Similarity floor 0.75 on the distinctive words (0.85 when a name is
  under four letters). "Ecco"/"Echo" is exactly 0.75.
- At most five confirmations per page; after the street list fails, it is
  not asked for again for two minutes.
- With no borough typed, the boroughs the geocoder answered with (at most
  two) are the ones searched.

Evidence
- tests/test_address_did_you_mean.py: 45 tests, all fetches mocked, an
  unexpected URL fails the test. Covers the measured typos, nonsense, the
  wrong borough, alias streets, the street-list failure, the geocode
  checks, both routes (links, query strings, escaping, status codes).
- Full suite on the Mac: 975 OK (1 skipped); 930 on main before.
- Live, through the real routes against the real services, on the Mac,
  2026-10-11:
    /area Ecco Place, Bronx        404, did you mean Echo Place, Bronx
    /area Westchster Ave, Bronx    404, Westchester Avenue, Bronx
    /area Grand Concorse, Bronx    404, Grand Concourse, Bronx
    /area Asdfgh Street, Bronx     404, no suggestions
    /area Echo Place, Brooklyn     404, Echo Place, Bronx first
    /building 231 Ecco Place, Bronx  404, 231 Echo Place, Bronx; Brown
                                     Place nowhere on the page
    /building 1 Echo Place, Bronx    200, notice, shows 179 Echo Place
    /building 231 Echo Place, Bronx  200, no notice
  Timing: 3.1 s for the first not-found search (cold street lists), 0.2 to
  1.6 s after.
- Headless Chrome on the Mac against the same live pages: light at
  1280 px, dark and light in a 390 px frame.

Not verified
- Keyboard: the suggestions are ordinary links with a visible focus ring
  in the stylesheet; tabbing to them was not driven by hand.
- Dark mode at desktop width was not captured (dark was seen at 390 px).
- Not checked on Render. The app token for the city's data service, if
  set there, was not exercised.

Known limits
- The street list covers streets with buildings under HPD's jurisdiction;
  a purely commercial street may be missing (brief's open question).
- Text with no commas, a neighbourhood name and no street type ("231 echo
  astoria") is treated as a different street and gets the not-found page.
- A name one letter from a real street can be offered though it isn't
  what the tenant meant ("Echo Place" in Brooklyn also offers Tech Place).
