What it can and cannot do

It can

  • Explain how NYC housing rules generally work
  • Point you to the right agency for your problem
  • Help you describe a situation clearly
  • Prepare a draft RA-81 complaint form
  • Keep a record of what you discussed

It cannot

  • Give you legal advice about your case
  • Represent you or file anything for you
  • Tell you how a judge will rule
  • Guarantee a rule still applies today
  • Replace a housing attorney

Where to get real help

All of these are free or low-cost. If your situation involves a court date, a signed document, or a deadline, start here rather than with us.

Your data

Your conversations are encrypted before they are stored, and only your account can read them back.
